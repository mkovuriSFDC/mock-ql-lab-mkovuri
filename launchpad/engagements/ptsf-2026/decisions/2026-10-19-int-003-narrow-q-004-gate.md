# Build INT-003 without the assessor/manager scope; Q-004 stays open

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Deciders:** madhuri (Trusted Guide) · **Intents:** INT-003

## Context

INT-003 (`intents/INT-003/intent.md`) is gated by Q-004, which is unanswered: can assessors work applications from other offices in their region, or only their own office? The question governs one build-target bullet (assessors and managers see applications within their office and region scope) and the acceptance sentence about the Singapore assessor. It does not touch the data model, patient sharing or practitioner sharing. INT-001 and INT-002 both need INT-003's objects, and every other phase-1 intent is also gated on an open question.

## Options

- **Wait for Q-004.** This blocks the phase-1 foundation that INT-001 and INT-002 build on.
- **Rule a provisional answer.** That would mean choosing a business rule PTSF hasn't stated.
- **Narrow the gate.** Build everything Q-004 doesn't govern, and hold the assessor/manager criteria as blocked.

## Choice

Narrow the gate. INT-003 builds the core objects, patient sharing and practitioner sharing now. The assessor/manager office-and-region visibility criteria go into the test script as `⛔ blocked` on Q-004. Q-004 stays open, and no intent text changes.

## Consequences

- INT-003 cannot be delivered (✅) until Q-004 is answered and the blocked criteria are built and proven, or until they are accepted as a gap by a named accepter.
- The internal sharing model (OWD, role hierarchy, office/region groups) must not lock in either answer to Q-004. The design keeps both options open.
